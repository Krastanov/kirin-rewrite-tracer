# Subsystem and Interface Contracts

No subsystem contracts are justified yet. Uniform events, their hierarchy, the ordinary
synchronous Python public-entry category, and the exact provenance boundary are
confirmed, including neutral incomplete events and selected statement-deletion effects,
but other callable forms, general state comparison, detailed unsupported-use failure
semantics, and stable capture-to-snapshot and snapshot-to-view interfaces remain
unresolved. The autonomous HTML artifact, pinned browser, native-control accessibility
model, fixed cascade, and parent-dominant tree-and-column interaction are confirmed
system boundaries, including narrow exact-equal handoff sharing and edge-scoped
provenance, but do not yet justify a subsystem topology.

Candidate responsibilities such as pinned integration, renderer-neutral snapshot
production, event and mutation recording, provenance projection, analysis, export
encoding, and browser presentation are discovery aids rather than normative boundaries.
