# Component Contracts

No component contracts are justified yet. The exact snapshot schema, style normalization
encoding, unsupported-use exception and partial-state rules, concrete identifier
representations, restoration guarantees, descriptor-wrapper topology, and concrete
provenance DTO encoding await subsystem decisions. The V-model nevertheless fixes
single-copy provenance semantics, both-direction lookup behavior, and the distinction
between incomplete operation shells and completed deletion effects. HTML/DOM structure
and embedded-data encoding remain private design choices subject to SYS-018 through
SYS-024.
